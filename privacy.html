Privacy Policy — Healthy Weight Goals

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information the Healthy Weight Goals app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information — the email address and sign-in details you use to create your account, so you can sign in and keep your records.
  • Health information you enter — the weight goals, weigh-ins, measurements, vital signs (blood pressure, blood glucose, heart rate, respiratory rate), lab values, and calculator results you choose to record in the app.

How we use it

Your information is used only to run the app for you: setting your calorie target, saving your entries, and showing your history, charts, and progress. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your health information for advertising.

Storage and security

Your records are stored with your account so they are available when you sign in. We use standard security measures, including encrypted connections, to protect data in transit.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete individual entries in the app at any time.
  • To delete your account and all data stored with it, email us at support@millieolaskitchen.com and we will complete the deletion.

Children

Healthy Weight Goals is intended for adults managing their own health goals and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version on this page and change the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@millieolaskitchen.com
