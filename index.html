About the app

Healthy Weight Goals turns your current weight, goal weight, and timeframe into a personalized daily calorie target, then keeps your health numbers in one place:

  • Weight goals with progress charts and statistics
  • Vital signs: blood pressure, blood glucose, heart rate, and respiratory rate
  • Lab values across panels including CBC, serum chemistry, cortisol, hemoglobin A1c, lipid panel, and GFR — each shown with its normal reference range
  • Built-in BMI and body fat calculators (U.S. Navy formula)

Frequently asked questions

How is my daily calorie target calculated?

When you set a goal, you enter your current weight, goal weight, and the timeframe you want to reach it in. The app turns those into a personal daily calorie target for your plan.

How do I record a weigh-in, vital sign, or lab result?

Open the matching section from the main menu (Weight, Vital Signs, or Lab Values), tap to add an entry, and save it with its date and time. Your history and charts update automatically.

Is Healthy Weight Goals a medical device?

No. Healthy Weight Goals is an educational and personal tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your physician about your health.
